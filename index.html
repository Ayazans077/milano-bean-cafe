Our Story

Where Every Cup
Tells a Story

Milano Bean Café brings the warmth of specialty coffee and the indulgence of artisan desserts to the heart of Downtown Syracuse. Nestled in the historic Pike Block, we're your neighborhood spot for expertly crafted drinks, freshly made food, and a space that feels like home.

Whether you're grabbing a quick pick-me-up or settling in for the night, we're open late and always happy to see you.
